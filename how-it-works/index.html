How it works

Need help nearby? Or want to earn nearby? Same app.

Barrtar is a peer-to-peer local task platform, live on Android in Mira Road, Dahisar, and Borivali. Post a task when you need help, or turn on availability and accept nearby tasks when you want to earn.

Post local tasks Earn nearby ₹0 platform fee 7:00 AM – 12:30 AM daily
  1. 1 Describe what you need, choose the location, add details, and set the amount.
  2. 2 Choose when: now, later today, tomorrow, or the day after.
  3. 3 Barrtar tries to match nearby available doers first, then widens the search if nobody accepts quickly.
  4. 4 Before sharing the start code, check that the person who arrived matches the accepted doer shown in Barrtar.
  5. 5 If the person does not match or something feels wrong, do not share the start code. Release, deny, or report the task from the app instead.
  6. 6 Coordinate directly, complete the task, and pay the doer directly once you're satisfied.

  2. 2 Turn on availability for match alerts, or browse nearby tasks directly. With background alerts enabled, you can get notified even when the app is closed.
  3. 3 Accept only what fits your time, distance, skills, and comfort.
  4. 4 Use the start code the creator shares once they've confirmed it's you, then begin the task.
  5. 5 Complete the task, share proof where needed, and receive payment directly from the creator.

Operating hours & scheduling

Barrtar operates from 7:00 AM to 12:30 AM every day. Posting, matching, and task alerts happen inside this window. Tasks can be scheduled for now, later today, tomorrow, or the day after — so help arrives exactly when you need it.

Post tasks you need done — not ads for services you provide. This keeps Barrtar a task marketplace, not a classifieds board. Read the community guidelines.

What Barrtar does not do

Barrtar does not charge users a platform fee, does not hold user money, and does not guarantee earnings, payment recovery, task completion, or perfect safety. Barrtar provides discovery, matching, coordination, verification gates, task evidence, and dispute support.
