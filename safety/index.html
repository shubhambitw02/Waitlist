Safety

Trust is built with checks, proof, and clear reporting.

Barrtar is live on Android in Mira Road, Dahisar, and Borivali, and safety keeps being tuned with real usage. The goal is simple: make local tasks easier without pretending every real-world risk disappears.

What is already part of the flow

ID verification for doers

Users can create tasks easily, but people accepting and working on tasks need ID verification first.

Task review and limits

Tasks can go through checks, and creators have active-task limits to reduce spam and misuse.

Direct-payment proof

Payments happen directly between users. Proof can be uploaded when useful for support or disputes.

Reports and disputes

Users can report problems and raise task disputes inside the app for review.

Start code check

Creators verify that the person who arrived matches the accepted doer before sharing the code that starts work (sent in-app as an OTP).

Operating hours

Barrtar runs from 7:00 AM to 12:30 AM, keeping posting, matching, and in-person handoffs inside a predictable daily window.

Safety expectations for users

  • Only post lawful, safe, clearly scoped tasks — not ads for services you provide.
  • Do not accept work that feels unsafe or outside your ability.
  • Agree scope, amount, timing, and proof before starting.
  • Creators should not share the start code until they are comfortable the matched person is the same person who arrived.
  • If the person does not match or something feels wrong, use release, deny, report, or support instead of starting the task.
  • Use in-app reports or support if something feels suspicious.
  • Do not share unnecessary sensitive information with other users.

Important limitation

Barrtar can add verification, checks, reports, evidence, and account action, but it cannot guarantee that every user, payment, task, or real-world interaction will be risk-free. Feedback from real usage keeps these controls improving as Barrtar expands to new areas.
