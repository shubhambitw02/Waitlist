FAQ

Quick answers about Barrtar.

Areas, hours, scheduling, earning, payments, the start code, and your account — everything in one place.

Live on Android · Borivali
Availability & hours

Barrtar is live on Android in Borivali, Mumbai, and we're expanding area by area. If you're not covered yet, you can join the waitlist when Barrtar reaches your area or iOS opens.

Barrtar runs from 7:00 AM to 12:30 AM every day. Posting, matching, and task alerts all operate inside this window.

Posting & scheduling

Yes. When you post a task, choose to get help now, later today, tomorrow, or the day after — whatever suits you.

Post tasks you need done — not ads for services you provide. Illegal, unsafe, exploitative, or deceptive work is never allowed, along with the categories listed in the Community Guidelines.

Earning & payments

Complete ID verification, then turn on Available for Work to get nearby task alerts. Accept tasks that fit your time and location, complete the work, and get paid directly by the creator.

Payments happen directly between the creator and the doer, such as by UPI or cash, once the task is agreed and completed. Barrtar does not charge a platform fee.

No. Barrtar does not hold user funds or process platform payments, and does not guarantee earnings, payment recovery, or task completion. See Terms for details.

Safety & alerts

Yes. With background alerts enabled, doers can receive nearby task alerts even when the app isn't open.

Tasks begin with a start code the creator shares with the doer. Before sharing it, the creator should check that the person who arrived matches the doer accepted in the app. If something feels wrong, don't share the code — use release, deny, report, or support instead. More at Safety.

Account

Open Barrtar, go to Profile, then Privacy and account, and choose account deletion. If you can't access the app, email support@barrtar.com with the subject "Account deletion request". Full process on the Account Deletion page.

Still have a question?

Email support@barrtar.com and we'll help directly.
